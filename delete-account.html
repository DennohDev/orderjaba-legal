Delete your account

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your OrderJaba account (the OrderJaba customer app, operated by Orderjaba, Nairobi, Kenya) and the personal data associated with it.

How to request deletion

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

  • Open the OrderJaba app and sign in.
  • Go to Menu → Settings.
  • Tap Delete account.
  • Confirm Delete account in the prompt.

Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Option 2 — By email

If you can’t access the app, email orderjaba@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my account”, from the email address on your account or including your registered phone number so we can verify you. We complete verified requests within 30 days. You can also call +254 704 180224.

What is deleted

When your account is deleted, we remove the personal data linked to it, including:

  • Your profile — name, email address, and phone number
  • Saved delivery addresses and map locations
  • Wishlist, saved preferences, and in-app messages
  • Device push-notification tokens

What is kept, and for how long

For legal, accounting, tax, and fraud-prevention reasons we may retain a limited record of your past orders and payment transactions (for example order totals and M-Pesa confirmation references). These records are kept as required by Kenyan tax and accounting law — generally up to 5 years — after which they are deleted or anonymised so they can no longer be linked to you. We may also retain information where we are legally required to, or to resolve a dispute.

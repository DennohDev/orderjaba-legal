Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how OrderJaba (“OrderJaba”, “we”, “us”, or “our”), operated by Orderjaba in Nairobi, Kenya, collects, uses, shares, and protects your personal data when you use the OrderJaba mobile apps, website, and delivery services (together, the “Service”).

We process personal data in accordance with the Data Protection Act, 2019 of Kenya and the regulations made under it. By using the Service, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Who we are

OrderJaba is a single-vendor Khat (miraa/jaba) and grocery delivery platform serving Nairobi and surrounding areas. The data controller is Orderjaba, contactable at orderjaba@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details — your first and last name, phone number, and email address.
  • Delivery details — delivery addresses, a map pin (coordinates) for your drop-off point, the recipient’s name and phone number, and any delivery notes you add.
  • Order details — the items you buy, order history, and any order notes.
  • Payment references — when you pay by M-Pesa, the M-Pesa confirmation code you enter so we can reconcile your payment. We never collect or store your M-Pesa PIN.
  • Communications — messages you send us through in-app chat or support channels.

Information collected automatically

  • Device & app data — device model, operating system, app version, and a push-notification token so we can send you order updates.
  • Location data — with your permission, your device location to help you set an accurate delivery pin and find nearby delivery areas. You can turn this off in your device settings.
  • Usage data — basic, non-identifying information about how you use the app to help us fix problems and improve the Service.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To process, deliver, and track your orders.
  • To verify M-Pesa payments against our records before dispatch.
  • To assign a delivery rider and share the delivery address and recipient contact so your order reaches you.
  • To send you order confirmations, status updates, and delivery notifications.
  • To provide customer support and respond to your enquiries.
  • To keep the Service secure, prevent fraud, and meet our legal obligations.
  • To improve our products, offers, and overall experience.

4. When we share your information

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only as needed to run the Service:

  • Delivery riders — your delivery address, map pin, recipient name, and phone number, so they can complete delivery.
  • Payment & mobile-money providers — Safaricom M-Pesa, to process and confirm payments.
  • Technology providers — for example Google (Maps and location services) and Google Firebase (push notifications and app infrastructure), who process data on our behalf under their own terms.
  • Authorities — where required by law, regulation, court order, or to protect our rights, users, or the public.

5. Age restriction

OrderJaba sells Khat (miraa/jaba), an adult product. The Service is intended for users 18 years and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us with data, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data retention

We keep your personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes above — including order history, accounting, and legal or tax requirements — after which we delete or anonymise it.

7. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019, you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your data is used.
  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request deletion of your data, where the law allows.
  • Object to, or request that we restrict, certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent (for example, location or notification permissions) at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email orderjaba@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC) of Kenya.

8. How we protect your data

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures — including encryption in transit, access controls, and encrypted storage of sensitive settings — to protect your data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep your information safe and to notify you and the ODPC of any serious breach as required by law.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version here and change the “Last updated” date above. Significant changes may also be notified in the app.
